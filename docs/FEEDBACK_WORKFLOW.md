# Feedback workflow policy

- Categories: service, driver, school, billing, application, suggestion, and urgent safety.
- Subjects are limited to 120 characters; messages and responses to 2,000 characters. Raw HTML is rejected.
- Users can view their own submission history and management replies. Submitted cases cannot be edited and users cannot append replies.
- Normal cases should receive an initial review within two working days. Safety cases are automatically marked urgent and escalated to the restricted super-admin queue for immediate operational handling.
- Only super-administrators can read, assign, respond to, or close cases. Every privileged read and state change is audited.
- Operational retention is 730 days after closure, subject to the organization’s final legal/privacy retention policy. Automated deletion is intentionally not enabled until that policy is approved.
